## Notes and touch vocabulary

- In landscape, Note Editor **replaces** the normal right column; it does not cover hands with a full-width Modal. Hands/header keep their geometry while IME is closed. Enter edit immediately when permitted, without automatically opening the software keyboard in landscape; tap the field to focus.
- Preserve one draft transaction keyed by stable authoritative `card.order`: saved-note delimiter, Save, Cancel/Back, draft-only Clear/quick actions, Repeat and upstream note parsing. Rotation/background preserves draft/editor. A departed card never retargets the draft to its replacement; switching cards must not silently discard unsaved work. Respect read-only and Rewind/replay permissions.
- Save/Cancel must remain usable under IME and system buttons. A measured temporary task presentation may be necessary under keyboard occlusion; hands are not guaranteed visible behind IME. Portrait editor behavior otherwise remains unchanged. No identity picker (#242).
- **No double-tap mode.** Own-card tap selects for separate explicit Play/Discard; own-card hold opens that card's note editor/inspection. Keep a discoverable explicit Note action on selection. Opponent-card tap opens editing immediately where permitted, otherwise read-only note inspection. Opponent-card/hand hold provides transient empathy until release. Own-card hold is reserved for notes; global empathy remains available.
- Recognized holds consume short taps. Release, cancellation, rotation, background and interruption clear temporary holds/empathy and never send gameplay commands. Preserve global empathy behavior, including turning it off on entering Clue.
- An explicit accessible `Clue` control beside each eligible opponent name targets/retargets the composer. Do not infer clue entry from card or player-row taps. Preserve turn/live/zero-token/pending gates and disabled legality. Labels and hit targets must fit without hand reflow.
- Candidate touched-card preview needs a legible high-contrast marker plus clear highlighting beyond the previous 8–11dp arrows. Preserve colorblind equivalence and distinct committed-clue, knowledge, note and identity channels.

## Home, replay exit and finishing

- Use visible `Home` consistently in focused Pregame, Live, own Rewind, finished/ordinary/shared Replay and Hypothetical. It always opens browsing Home, preserves attendance/runtime and retains Return; it never means the previous browsing destination.
- Provide direct one-tap `Exit Replay` for ordinary/finished/shared replay, with **no confirmation**, including shared leaders. Keep destructive Terminate confirmation unchanged. Own ongoing Rewind uses `Live`, never replay exit or table-unattend.
- Actual attended review exit uses existing authoritative attendance eligibility, current table/load/connection ownership and duplicate-send protection. Restore supported browsing origin (History when applicable, otherwise Home) on authoritative departure. Failed send/disconnect must not falsely claim exit. No implicit termination, stale retarget, gameplay send or command retry.
- No separate results page or persistent extra result banners. On a real observed active-game → authoritative-finished transition, show one restrained accessible `Game finished` popup with authoritative score/max, accurate reason and `Continue to replay` dismissal. No command or automatic rematch.
- Do not show the popup on already-finished hydration, historical/imported replay, reconnect reattachment, rotation, Home/Return, repeated frames or timeline scrubbing. Track current room/table/game lifetime; a genuinely ending new game/rematch is separate. Preserve ordinary historical Last Action and necessary Replay/turn/shared anchors; remove only redundant endgame clutter.
